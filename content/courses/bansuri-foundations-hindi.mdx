# What you'll learn

This free YouTube series is designed for absolute beginners. By the end, you should be able to hold the bansuri correctly, produce a clean tone, and play a few simple compositions in the lower octave.

## Topics covered

- **Posture & grip** — how to hold the flute without tension, where the fingers sit, how to angle the embouchure.
- **Breath control** — diaphragmatic breathing, sustaining a note, the difference between blowing air and shaping it.
- **First notes** — Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni and how to find them cleanly.
- **Octave transitions** — how breath pressure changes between mandra (low) and madhya (middle) saptak.
- **Simple compositions** — short lehras and beginner-friendly bandishes you can play along with.

## Who this is for

If you have just bought your first bansuri (or are about to), this is the right place to start. No prior music training is assumed. A C natural medium or G base bansuri will work for most lessons.

## How to practise alongside

Use the free [practice tools on the home page](/) — the metronome and taanpura drone will keep your sense of time and tonic steady while you work through the lessons. Twenty focused minutes a day, every day, will take you further than two hours once a week.
